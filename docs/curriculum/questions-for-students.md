# The Autonomy Protocol: Student Self-Assessment

Evaluating personal autonomy requires assessing whether an individual maintains self-governance or relies on external dependencies that strip away personal freedom and self-determination. The following assessment is designed for high school freshmen and seniors to evaluate individual responsibility, self-ownership, and decision-making clarity.

---

## Self-Governance & Control

- [ ] **Habit Control:** Have you tried to cut back on a substance or habit, only to find yourself breaking your own rules?
- [ ] **Daily Reliance:** Do you rely on a substance, vape, or external habit to wake up, get through the school day, or fall asleep?
- [ ] **Dependency Anxiety:** Do you feel anxious, panicked, or irritable at the thought of going without a specific substance or habit?
- [ ] **Social Independence:** Do you believe you cannot socialize, fit in, or have a good time without using something to alter your state of mind?

## Personal Responsibility & Accountability

- [ ] **Performance Impact:** Have your grades, athletic performance, or job responsibilities suffered because of your choices?
- [ ] **Financial Stewardship:** Have you spent money intended for savings, gas, or necessities on short-term habits or substances?
- [ ] **Honesty & Transparency:** Have you lied to your parents, teachers, or friends about what you are doing or how often you do it?
- [ ] **Integrity:** Have you resorted to hiding things, stealing, or manipulating others to maintain a habit?

## Emotional Reliance & Relationships

- [ ] **Coping Mechanisms:** Do you turn to substances or external distractions as your primary way to handle stress, anger, or tough situations?
- [ ] **Association & Environment:** Do you avoid certain friends, family members, or activities because they do not approve of your choices?
- [ ] **Internal Alignment:** Do you feel defensive, guilty, or secretive when someone asks you about your habits?
- [ ] **Family & Home Impact:** Are your choices creating conflict, mistrust, or disruption at home with your family?

## Self-Ownership & Freedom

- [ ] **Consequence Recognition:** Do you continue a habit even after it has caused clear negative consequences in your life?
- [ ] **Risk Assessment:** Have you taken unnecessary risks or used things without knowing what they were or how they would affect you?
- [ ] **External Outcomes:** Have your choices led to disciplinary action at school, legal trouble, or medical issues?
- [ ] **Core Assessment:** Looking at your life honestly, are you in total control of your decisions, or are your habits controlling you?

---

### Purpose & Next Steps
This self-assessment helps students evaluate personal autonomy, self-governance, and voluntary control over their choices. If responses indicate that external dependencies are hindering self-mastery, *The Autonomy Protocol* provides structured framework modules to rebuild independence and personal accountability.
